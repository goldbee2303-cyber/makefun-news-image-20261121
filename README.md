Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 21 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121).

A dense newspaper-style image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026 — 12 stories on one page: a navy masthead with the date, a 6-stat strip, three illustrated lead stories, four cards plus a full-width volcano strip, a numbered briefs column, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map and a "DATE CHECK" footer. Every story carries its source beside it, and each brief's number is coloured with its region colour, matching the map.

Leads: a M6.9 earthquake shakes Peru's Andes (no deaths, no injuries); Russia fires 148 drones at Ukraine overnight (117 downed, 2 killed); COP31 talks run into a second day of overtime in Antalya with still no deal. Cards: the FBI probes a suspected hack of the NCIC crime database; a fire at the Hartha power plant near Basra kills at least 4; India reach 486/4 at stumps on Day 3 in Wellington, leading by 118; SpaceX launches the classified USSF-317 payload from Vandenberg. Strip: Sakurajima erupts in Kagoshima, ash 1,000–1,200 m, its 35th eruption of 2026. Briefs: Vietnam's land law passes 479–5; Iran claims a 58th US MQ-9 shootdown over Hormuz; North Melbourne beat Sydney 83–51 in the AFLW preliminary final; the ATP Finals semis in Turin are Alcaraz v Shelton and Zverev v Medvedev.

Built in the dense front-page style of the 20 November entry — 6-stat strip, detailed flat illustrations per story, region-coloured brief numbers, numbered map pins and a date check — using only verified facts. The image is drawn as a front page and the program copies it to the output directory unchanged.

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. The image is 1024×1024 PNG.
